How to print & fold

  1. Print double-sided, flipping on the short edge.
  2. Choose Landscape and Letter paper.
  3. Leave Margins on Default.
  4. Set scale to 100% (not "Fit to page").
  5. Stack the sheets in order, fold the whole stack in half along the dotted line, and staple the spine.

Sheets below are shown in printing order, not reading order — that is expected. Each sheet's front and back are labelled.

Print one sheet first to check. Drivers label duplex differently: if the back comes out upside-down, switch to long edge.

The book prints black and white, with no date, URL or page number in the margins — you do not need to change any of those settings.
